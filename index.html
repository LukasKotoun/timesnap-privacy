TimeSnap – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

TimeSnap is a voice-first daily time log for Android, made as a student project at the Faculty of Information Technology, Brno University of Technology (FIT VUT). This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes and how you can delete it. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell data.

Data stored on your phone

  • Your time entries, categories, daily goals, items waiting in "To resolve", saved AI examples and settings.
  • For each voice recording: its transcript and the AI answer, so you can see what an entry was made from.
  • Your own Groq API key, if you enter one. It is stored encrypted and is never included in backups.

This data stays on your phone. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include it in your device backup (the API key is excluded). Backups and CSV exports made in the app are saved only where you choose.

Microphone and voice recordings

The app records audio only after you tap a microphone button (in the app, in the notification or in the widget). To turn the recording into entries, the app sends:

  • the audio to Groq for speech-to-text, and
  • the transcript to Groq's language model, together with the context it needs: the current date and time, your category names and voice keywords, recently used activity names, the entries of that day and your saved AI examples.

Data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The audio file is deleted from your phone right after it has been processed; without internet it waits on the phone until it can be sent. Groq processes the data under its own privacy policy.

Weekly insight

If you entered your own Groq API key, the app sends a summary of the past week to Groq once a week to get suggestions ("What could change"): time per category, unlogged time, the most frequent activities and your goals. Without your own key this summary is never sent.

Phone usage (optional)

If you turn on "Show screen time" and allow usage access, the app reads when your screen was on, to draw it in the day dial. This information stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

Notifications

Notifications are used only for reminders and the quick-capture notification you turn on in Settings › Notifications.

Deleting your data

You can delete single entries in the app, or all data by uninstalling the app or by clearing its storage in Android settings. Nothing is kept on any server of ours – we do not run one.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
